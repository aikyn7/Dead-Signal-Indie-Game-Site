Terminal 2: Known Entities & Tactics

Entity Threat Level Survival Protocol
The Tall Man FATAL Unkillable. Do not engage. Break line of sight immediately. Hide in closets or under beds.
Flesh Crawlers HIGH Fast and aggressive. Wait for them to leap, then use 1-2 shotgun shells at close range.
Wall Shadows MEDIUM Drains sanity if observed. Keep your flashlight on and do not look directly at them.
