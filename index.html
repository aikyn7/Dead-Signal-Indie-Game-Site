A downloadable game for Windows

DEAD SIGNAL is a short, terrifying survival horror experience inspired by classic PSX-era games.

You find yourself trapped in a decaying, nightmarish reality. The environment is hostile, resources are scarce, and something is hunting you through the dark forests and abandoned hospital corridors. Manage your shotgun ammo carefully, solve puzzles, and try to survive the unexplainable horrors that lurk in the static.
