Station 17

"Some signals should never be found."

📻 THE SIGNAL

In 1997, a secret radio station known as Station 17 was built in an isolated forest zone. Officially, the facility belonged to a private research company studying atmospheric radio signals. But the station's true purpose was different.

Scientists discovered a strange radio signal appearing every night on the same frequency. It sounded neither like human speech nor ordinary static. The most disturbing part? The signal responded to human voices.

Weeks later, personnel began reporting inexplicable events:

  • they heard the voices of dead relatives through the radio;
  • security cameras occasionally captured people who were not on site;
  • employees vanished after night shifts;
  • massive footprints were found deep in the surrounding forest.

🧪 EXPERIMENT 17

The company ordered the research to continue. The longer a subject listened to the signal, the more frequently they encountered the exact same entity—a tall, emaciated figure with disproportionately long arms. In classified documents, he was referred to as THE TALL MAN.

Management decided to test whether physical interaction with the signal was possible, constructing an underground laboratory beneath the station. Subjects, officially listed as volunteers, were placed in isolated rooms and forced to listen to SIGNAL 0. Their true identities remain unknown.

Days later, some began hearing a voice. One of the final recorded transcripts stated:

“It knows we're listening.”

Shortly after, all communication with the laboratory was lost.

🔴 INCIDENT 17 & 27 YEARS LATER

On the night of October 17, 1998, Station 17 simultaneously disconnected from the outside world. The radio frequency violently spiked. For a few brief seconds, the entire facility broadcasted a single, synchronized phrase:

“LET US OUT.”

When the company's response team arrived the next morning, they found everyone dead. Several bodies were missing entirely. The company sealed the facility, declared it destroyed by fire, and erased all records.

In 2025, a local amateur radio operator accidentally stumbles upon the dead frequency. SIGNAL 0 has returned, but this time, it transmits coordinates leading straight to the abandoned ruins of Station 17. The protagonist goes there, believing he is searching for missing people. He is wrong.
